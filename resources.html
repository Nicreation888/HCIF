What we fund

Funding priorities

Projects should primarily align with one of the following six areas. Applicants do not need to address every priority.

Education

  • Improve educational outcomes
  • Support literacy
  • Increase college access
  • Provide after-school programming
  • Promote lifelong learning

Workforce Development

  • Prepare individuals for employment
  • Support career advancement
  • Develop technical skills
  • Connect employers and job seekers
  • Reduce employment barriers

Health & Wellness

  • Improve physical or mental health
  • Increase healthcare access
  • Promote healthy lifestyles
  • Address substance misuse
  • Improve food security

Community Development

  • Strengthen neighborhoods
  • Improve housing stability
  • Promote civic engagement
  • Build community partnerships
  • Increase public safety

Technology & Digital Access

  • Expand broadband access
  • Improve digital literacy
  • Reduce technology inequities
  • Prepare individuals for technology careers

Environmental Sustainability

  • Improve environmental stewardship
  • Promote conservation
  • Create green workforce opportunities
  • Improve community resilience
