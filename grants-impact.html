Beyond the award

Impact includes what partners learn.

HCIF asks partners to measure progress, use findings to strengthen implementation, and share practical lessons from the work.

We value both demonstrated results and thoughtful adaptation. Strong evaluation helps partners understand what worked, for whom, under what conditions, and what should happen next.
