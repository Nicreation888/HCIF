About HCIF

Community knowledge. Practical investment. Lasting value.

The Horizon Community Impact Foundation partners with nonprofit organizations working to expand opportunity and strengthen well-being across the United States.

Our mission

To invest in community-informed nonprofit initiatives that expand opportunity, strengthen well-being, and produce meaningful, measurable benefits.

Our vision

Thriving communities where all people have equitable access to the opportunities, resources, and support they need to succeed.

How we work

We fund with communities, not simply for them.

HCIF was established around a straightforward idea: durable change is more likely when solutions reflect local knowledge, trusted relationships, and a clear understanding of the need.

We seek nonprofit partners that listen carefully, design responsibly, and connect activities to outcomes that matter. Our role is to provide flexible project support, ask useful questions, and promote learning that can extend beyond a single grant period.

We do not prescribe one model. We look for alignment, evidence, feasibility, responsible budgets, and a credible path to lasting community value.

Our values

What guides our decisions

Community voice

We value the knowledge, priorities, and lived experience of the people closest to the work.

Responsible stewardship

We expect resources to be managed carefully, transparently, and in direct service of intended results.

Collaboration

We support partnerships that bring complementary strengths to complex community challenges.

Measurable benefit

We look for meaningful outcomes, practical learning, and evidence that communities are better served.
