About HCIF

Investing in opportunity since 2011

HCIF is a private philanthropic organization established by former business leaders committed to strengthening communities.

Who we are

The Horizon Community Impact Foundation believes lasting community change occurs when nonprofit organizations address root causes rather than short-term symptoms. We invest in evidence-informed, community-driven programs that improve quality of life and increase access to opportunity for historically underserved populations.

Mission

To strengthen communities by investing in nonprofit organizations that expand opportunity, improve well-being, and create sustainable solutions to local challenges.

Vision

Communities where every individual has equitable access to education, economic opportunity, health, and civic participation.

Core values

  • Collaboration
  • Innovation
  • Equity
  • Accountability
  • Sustainability
  • Community Voice
  • Measurable Impact
