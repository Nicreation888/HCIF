FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clarifications about eligibility, allowable costs, and the application process.

Can a university apply?

A university may apply through an eligible nonprofit unit that meets all applicant requirements.

Can a faith-based organization apply?

Yes, provided the funded activities are nonsectarian and do not support religious instruction or worship.

Are partnerships required?

Partnerships are encouraged for all proposals and required for Strategic Partnership Grants.

Can grant funds support salaries?

Yes. Personnel costs must be reasonable, directly related to the project, and clearly explained in the budget justification.

Are indirect costs allowed?

Indirect costs are allowed up to 10% of the requested amount.

Can an existing program be funded?

Yes, when the proposal demonstrates expansion, adaptation, increased access, or improved impact.

Can equipment be purchased?

Equipment is allowable when necessary for program delivery, but requests consisting mainly of equipment are generally not competitive.

May an organization submit more than one proposal?

No. Each organization may submit one proposal per annual cycle.
