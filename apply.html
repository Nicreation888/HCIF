How to apply

A two-stage process designed to begin with fit.

All applicants submit a brief Letter of Inquiry. Organizations with eligible, aligned, and ready concepts may be invited to develop a full proposal.

Download the RFP
1

Due August 15 annually

Letter of Inquiry

Maximum two pages

HCIF uses the LOI to assess basic eligibility, alignment, and organizational readiness before requesting a full proposal.

  • Applicant name, mission, nonprofit status, and contact
  • Community need and population to be served
  • Project summary, anticipated outcomes, and approximate request
  • Mission and primary-priority alignment
  • Organizational capacity and significant partners
Invitation decisions by September 1
2

Due September 30 annually

Full Proposal

Narrative maximum seven pages

Invited applicants submit the full narrative, budget materials, timeline, logic model, and required attachments by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time.

An invitation to submit a full proposal does not guarantee funding.

Full proposal

Required application components

Use clear headings that correspond to these components. The narrative page limit excludes the cover page, executive summary, budget materials, timeline, logic model, references, and attachments.

01Cover page

Applicant and project details, funding priority, request amount, project period, and authorized representative.

02Executive summary

A concise overview of the need, response, intended participants, amount requested, and expected results. Maximum 300 words.

03Organizational background and capacity

Mission, relevant experience, population served, staff and partner roles, and capacity to administer the project and grant funds.

04Statement of need

Credible evidence defining the need, affected population, contributing conditions, service gaps, and urgency.

05Project design

Goals, measurable objectives or outcomes, activities, implementation approach, responsibilities, partnerships, and timeline.

06Evaluation plan

Indicators, data sources, collection methods, responsibilities, and how findings will be used.

07Budget and budget narrative

A complete project budget showing requested and other resources, with a clear justification for each cost.

08Sustainability

How benefits, effective practices, partnerships, or essential activities may continue after HCIF funding ends.

09Required attachments

Project timeline, logic model, board list, IRS determination letter or fiscal-sponsor documentation, and other specified materials.

Submission standards

Present a complete, professional application

  • Use an accessible professional typeface of at least 11 points
  • Use margins of at least one inch
  • Cite external data using a consistent professional style
  • Submit every required component by the applicable deadline
  • Review the complete RFP before submission

Late or substantially incomplete applications will not be reviewed. If a deadline falls on a weekend or U.S. federal holiday, it moves to the next business day.
