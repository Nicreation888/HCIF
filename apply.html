How to apply

A two-stage process designed to begin with fit.

All applicants submit a brief Letter of Inquiry. Organizations with eligible, aligned, and ready concepts may be invited to develop a full proposal.

Download the RFP ↓
1
LOI

Due August 15 annually

Letter of Inquiry

Maximum two pages

HCIF uses the LOI to assess basic eligibility, alignment, and organizational readiness before requesting a full proposal.

  • ✓ Applicant name, mission, nonprofit status, and contact
  • ✓ Community need and population to be served
  • ✓ Project summary, anticipated outcomes, and approximate request
  • ✓ Mission and primary-priority alignment
  • ✓ Organizational capacity and significant partners
Invitation decisions by September 1→
2
FP

Due September 30 annually

Full Proposal

Narrative maximum seven pages

Invited applicants submit the full narrative, budget materials, timeline, logic model, and required attachments by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time.

An invitation to submit a full proposal does not guarantee funding.

Full proposal

Required application components

Use clear headings that correspond to these professional proposal components. The narrative page limit excludes the cover letter, executive summary, budget materials, timeline, logic model, references, and attachments.

01Cover letter⌄

A brief letter introducing the organization and project, identifying the amount requested, and summarizing why the proposal is a strong fit with HCIF.

02Executive summary⌄

A concise overview of the full proposal, including the need, proposed response, intended participants, amount requested, and expected results. Maximum 300 words.

03Problem statement⌄

A compelling, evidence-based description of the community need the project will address, including the affected population, contributing conditions, service gaps, and urgency.

04Organizational background⌄

The organization’s mission, qualifications, relevant experience, population served, staff and partner roles, and capacity to administer the project and grant funds.

05Goals and objectives⌄

The project’s broad intended results and the specific, measurable outcomes the organization expects to achieve during the grant period.

06Strategies⌄

The programs, services, activities, partnerships, responsibilities, and implementation timeline that will be used to achieve the proposed results.

07Evaluation⌄

The plan for assessing program accomplishments, including indicators, data sources, collection methods, responsibilities, and use of findings.

08Sustainability⌄

The organization’s strategy for continuing project benefits, effective practices, partnerships, or essential activities after the HCIF grant period.

09Budget⌄

A complete project budget and budget narrative showing requested and other resources, with a clear justification for each cost.

Required attachments: Project timeline, logic model, board list, IRS determination letter or fiscal-sponsor documentation, and any other materials specified in the RFP.

Submission standards

Present a complete, professional application

  • ✓ Use an accessible professional typeface of at least 11 points
  • ✓ Use margins of at least one inch
  • ✓ Cite external data using a consistent professional style
  • ✓ Submit every required component by the applicable deadline
  • ✓ Review the complete RFP before submission

Late or substantially incomplete applications will not be reviewed. If a deadline falls on a weekend or U.S. federal holiday, it moves to the next business day.
