Frequently asked questions

Answers for prospective applicants.

Review the complete RFP together with these answers. FAQ guidance does not replace the requirements stated in the funding opportunity.

Community Impact Grant

Before you begin

Confirm eligibility, identify one primary funding priority, and use the next available annual cycle.

Review the opportunity →
Which annual cycle should an applicant use?⌄

Use the next available September 30 full-proposal deadline. Letter of Inquiry submissions are due August 15 for that cycle.

May a project address more than one funding priority?⌄

Yes. Applicants must identify one primary priority and explain where the project has its strongest alignment. Meaningful connections to additional priorities may also be described.

Is a funding match required?⌄

No. Matching funds are not required. Applicants should include any other committed or pending resources in the complete project budget.

May grant funds support indirect costs?⌄

Yes. Administrative or indirect costs may not exceed 10% of the amount requested from HCIF.

Does an invitation to submit a full proposal guarantee funding?⌄

No. An invitation means that the project may advance to the full-proposal stage. It is not a promise of an award.

Can a public agency apply?⌄

Yes, when it applies in formal partnership with an eligible 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

Can a community-based organization use a fiscal sponsor?⌄

Yes. The fiscal sponsor must be an eligible 501(c)(3) organization that accepts fiduciary and grant-management responsibility.

What happens when a deadline falls on a weekend or federal holiday?⌄

The deadline moves to the next business day. Submissions are due by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the applicable deadline.

Are the website dates the same as course assignment deadlines?⌄

The dates published on this website represent HCIF’s fictional annual funding cycle. Students participating in the academic simulation should follow the submission dates established by their instructor and shown in the course schedule.
