Application guidance

Use the information below to determine whether your organization and proposed project are eligible and competitive.

Eligibility

  • Registered nonprofit organization
  • Operating for at least two years
  • Demonstrates financial accountability
  • Serves communities in the United States
  • Has a governing board
  • Can measure program outcomes

Award levels

Grant typeAmountBest fit

What we generally do not fund

  • Political campaigns
  • Religious activities
  • Individual scholarships
  • Endowments
  • Annual fundraising events
  • Debt reduction
  • Deficit financing
  • International projects
  • Organizations focused primarily on animal welfare
  • Capital campaigns exceeding 00,000
  • Projects beginning before the award date
  • Requests consisting mainly of equipment purchases
  • Programs duplicating existing services without evidence of unmet need
