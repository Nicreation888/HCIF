What we fund

Investment shaped around the conditions communities need to thrive.

Our six priorities reflect interconnected dimensions of opportunity and well-being. Every proposal identifies one primary priority while recognizing where the work may connect across fields.

01

Learning that opens doors

Education

Literacy, college access, after-school learning, and lifelong-learning opportunities.

We support programs that strengthen foundational skills, remove barriers to education, and help learners of all ages pursue meaningful pathways.

02

Skills that lead to opportunity

Workforce Development

Career preparation, technical skills, advancement, and access to employment.

We invest in practical career pathways, employer-informed training, and supports that connect people to sustainable employment.

03

Conditions that help people thrive

Health & Wellness

Health-care access, physical and mental health, food security, and healthy lifestyles.

We fund community-based approaches that expand access, reduce barriers, and strengthen physical and emotional well-being.

04

Strong places and connected neighbors

Community Development

Housing stability, civic engagement, partnerships, public safety, and neighborhood strength.

We support resident-informed solutions that strengthen local capacity, belonging, safety, and long-term neighborhood stability.

05

Digital access for full participation

Technology & Digital Access

Broadband access, digital literacy, technology equity, and technology careers.

We invest in access, skills, devices, and pathways that help communities participate confidently in a digital society.

06

Resilient communities and shared resources

Environmental Sustainability

Conservation, environmental stewardship, community resilience, and green-workforce opportunities.

We fund practical efforts that protect local resources, prepare communities for change, and create inclusive green-economy pathways.

Our funding lens

What we look for across every priority

  • A need defined with specific, credible evidence
  • A response shaped by the community it will serve
  • A feasible design connecting activities and outcomes
  • Appropriate partnerships and organizational capacity
  • A responsible budget and plan for lasting value
