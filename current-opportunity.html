Community Impact Grant

Opportunity overview

Support for a focused project with a credible path to results

Competitive proposals demonstrate a strong connection among the identified need, proposed activities, people who will benefit, and expected outcomes.

Each proposal identifies one primary funding priority. Projects may connect to additional priorities when those relationships are meaningful and clearly explained.

HCIF may award less than the amount requested and may request clarification or budget adjustments before making a final award.

Eligibility

Who may apply

Eligible lead applicants

Meet one of these conditions

  • ✓ A nonprofit organization recognized as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) and in good standing
  • ✓ A public agency applying in formal partnership with an eligible 501(c)(3) nonprofit
  • ✓ A community-based organization applying through an eligible 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor
Project requirements

Demonstrate readiness and fit

  • ✓ Primarily benefit a U.S. community or population
  • ✓ Respond to a clearly documented community need
  • ✓ Align with one primary HCIF funding priority
  • ✓ Show capacity to manage the project, award, and reporting

Ineligible applicants and activities

Individuals, for-profit businesses, partisan activity or lobbying, religious instruction, endowments, debt retirement, fundraising events, regranting, pre-award costs, and major construction or property acquisition are not eligible.

Annual funding cycle
